# Provenance and release scope

Original project materials were inspected in the AYA project folders: the February 2025 research report, presentation narrative, six full firmware sketches, the `aya-yz` code/data folder, prototype media and the ceiling enclosure STL. A March 2026 project summary was consulted for context. Duplicate files, operating-system metadata, partial code fragments, private administrative records, participant photographs, third-party disaster footage and literature PDFs are not redistributed.

The original `.ino` filenames and source hashes are recorded in `source_manifest.json`. Published archive files have been renamed into matching sketch folders and cleaned of embedded service credentials and tutorial comments. Their historical behaviour and known limitations are documented in `archive/README.md`.

The standalone Python analysis, C++ maintenance logic, tests and repository documentation were prepared in October 2026. They are additions for reproducibility and portfolio maintenance, not evidence of what ran during the 2025 competition.

Prototype photographs and the selected mobile screenshots are extracted from the team's February 2025 report. The single STL is preserved byte-for-byte.
